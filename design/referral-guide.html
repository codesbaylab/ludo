🎁 Referral Guide

How it works

  1. Go Pro (one-time fee, today $20). Only Pro members get a referral link — find it in Profile → Refer & Earn.
  2. Share your link (login.html?ref=YOURCODE). Friends who sign up through it are tied to you for good.
  3. They go Pro — the moment a friend pays the Pro fee, commissions are paid instantly into the wallets of the people above them in the chain.
  4. Money you can use — commissions land in your wallet balance: play, transfer or withdraw.

Commission is paid on the Pro fee only — one time per person. It is not a share of anyone's game winnings or losses.

Rules to know

  • An upline must be Pro and not banned to receive a level's commission. A non-Pro person in the chain earns nothing for their level, and the chain continues past them.
  • Each Pro purchase pays each level once (no double payouts).
  • Granting Pro manually (by admin) pays no commissions.
  • Fake or self-referral accounts can be frozen or banned by the risk engine; keep it genuine.
